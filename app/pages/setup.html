Create New Bot
Step-by-step guide to create your own maintenance bot

⚠ Never share your bot token — treat it like a password.

Your bot must be in the server before submitting. It needs Administrator permission.

1
Open the Discord Developer Portal

Log in with your Discord account, then click New Application.

Open Developer Portal ↗
2
Name your application

Use something recognizable, e.g. MyServer-Bot. Then click Create.

3
Add a Bot user

Go to the Bot tab → click Add Bot. Enable Server Members Intent and Message Content Intent.

4
Copy your Bot Token

Click Reset Token → confirm → paste it below.

⚠ Never share your token publicly.

5
Invite your bot to your server

After verifying the token, generate an invite link and add the bot before submitting.
