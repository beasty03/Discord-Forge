Frequently Asked Questions

What does "runs on your PC" mean for the free plan? ▼
On the free plan, your Discord bots run as a process on your own computer. You download a ZIP package from the Bots page and launch it locally. The bot only works while your computer is on and the process is running. Paid plans let you host bots in our cloud so they run 24/7 without your PC.
Can I switch from cloud to self-hosted on a paid plan? ▼
Yes. Pro and Enterprise users can freely switch each bot between cloud hosting and the local download at any time from the Bots page.
What are "premium scripts"? ▼
Premium scripts are advanced bot modules — things like advanced moderation, levelling systems, ticket systems, and integrations. Free scripts cover the essentials. Premium scripts are available exclusively on Pro and Enterprise plans.
