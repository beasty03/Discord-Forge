🤖 Add a Bot

Connect a Discord bot to one of your servers

1 · Create on Discord
2 · Paste Token
3 · Invite to Server

First, create a bot in the Discord Developer Portal.

1
Open the Developer Portal
Click the button below.
2
Click "New Application"
Give it a name — this becomes your bot's name in Discord.
3
Go to Bot → "Reset Token"
Copy the token — you'll need it in the next step.
4
Enable "Message Content Intent"
Bot → Privileged Gateway Intents → turn it on.
🔗 Open Developer Portal
Developer Portal → Your App → Bot → Token

Invite your bot to the selected Discord server so it can be used.

The invite link is generated from your bot token. Click below to open the Discord authorisation page.

🔗 Open Invite Page in Discord

You can also save without inviting and invite manually later.
